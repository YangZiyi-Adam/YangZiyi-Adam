# Hi, I'm Ziyi Yang 👋

I'm an Honours BSc student in **Mathematics and Physics at the University of Toronto**. I work across quantum computing, many-body physics, and data-driven modeling of physical systems. I enjoy connecting mathematical ideas with simulations, experiments, and practical analysis.

## What I'm working on

- **Quantum simulation of 1D spin chains:** I study real-time dynamics and coherence using matrix product states and quantum circuits. My recent work explores shallow circuit designs and machine-learning-guided gate allocation for failure-aware simulation on noisy quantum devices.
- **Data-driven composite materials research:** I analyze fatigue and delamination in tapered carbon-fibre-reinforced polymer (CFRP) laminates. My work uses stress-conditioned risk models, grouped validation across specimens, and comparisons between classical machine learning and quantum kernel methods.
